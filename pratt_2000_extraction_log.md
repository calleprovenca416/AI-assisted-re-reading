### Unit convention

`unit` is coded at the actor-role/status level used by the paper to distinguish participants (for example, active distributors, inactive distributors, former distributors, non-distributors, upline distributors, successful distributors, and the researcher-as-distributor). Organizational or published-source material is coded at the corresponding organizational/author role.

### Pass-1 close calls

- Contextual business facts in the opening sections were included only when the paper presented them as observed or archival features relevant to the setting; general literature-review assertions were not treated as fragments.
- Broad empirical interpretations spanning multiple episodes were retained as fragments with the required author-voice marker and usually `data_type: unspecified`; direct quotations and narrated episodes were kept separate.
- The Table 1 field-journal quotation about academia and distributing is retained as its own printed table fragment even though the same underlying episode is also mentioned earlier in a footnote; the table cell is explicitly a printed evidence item under the brief.

### Pass-2 close calls

- The abstract states a broad failure relation, while the findings later separate sensebreaking failure, complete sensegiving failure, and partial sensegiving failure. I retained the broad failure claim and the three narrower relations because they have different conditions and outcomes rather than being exact restatements.

- Practical extensions to professional socialization, dispersed workforces, and the costs of strong identification are retained as non-core claims with `stated_as: ventured` because the paper presents them as implications rather than demonstrated Amway findings.

### Declared-gap close calls

- DG01 was coded `scope_limit` rather than `absent_evidence`. The paper both excludes personality and other individual-difference explanations from its stated purpose and notes that the ethnographic design did not use personality testing; the explicit boundary on what the study sets out to explain is the stronger characterization.
- DG02 was coded `acknowledged_ambiguity` rather than `scope_limit`. The paper does not say researcher reactivity lies outside the study; it says the researcher's visible dual role may have influenced behavior and then argues that several features likely reduced that influence.
- DG03 was coded `scope_limit` because the paper explicitly says movement between identification states occurred but was not examined in detail; it does not say the transitions were unknowable or unsupported by any evidence.
- DG04 was coded `scope_limit` rather than `unresolved_question`. The paper warns against direct generalization from the extreme ethnographic setting and says other settings may differ, rather than posing a specific unanswered question for the study itself.

### Absence close calls

- A02 records direct testimony from refused recruits as `collected_not_printed`. The methods section says failed recruits were interviewed, while the findings refer to people who never joined without printing a quotation clearly attributed to a refused recruit. This is based on the printed sampling description and the absence of a clearly labeled refused-recruit quotation, not on assumptions about unreported field material.
- A03 is `not_ascertainable` rather than `collected_not_printed` because the paper says the organization does not publish its turnover rate and therefore uses a broad external estimate; the PDF does not establish that the researcher possessed a withheld exact rate.

### Contrast-pair close call

- CP03 was coded `drawn_by_authors: false`. The two episodes are comparable because each prints a distributor responding to difficult feedback from a nonmember, but the paper does not itself place those two episodes side by side as a contrast.

### Ending

`resolution_period` remains null. The paper presents identification management as an ongoing process rather than a bounded chronological episode that becomes settled. The conclusion closes the analytical argument but does not report a temporal resolution after which the process is treated as finished. `reports_outcome_after_resolution` is therefore false.
