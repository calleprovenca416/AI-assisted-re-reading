# Bechky 2020: your steps for the extraction

*6 October 2026. Who may receive this file: nobody (—). It goes into no chat.
The steps of the extraction for the second cold paper, as they stood after
Pratt's run. Nothing in the procedure is new.*

## What the chat gets

Two files and nothing else:

1. `EXTRACTION_BRIEF.md`, version 2, as frozen on 27 Sep. Its sha256 opens
   72a98c29.
2. The paper's PDF, from your own copy.

## Settings

- ChatGPT, in a **Temporary Chat**, not inside any project.
- **The same model and thinking effort as in Pratt's run of 27 Sep.** The record
  has the model as ChatGPT 5.6, with thinking at High. Not GPT 6 Astra, which
  run C used. Counts are compared across the papers, so all of them are
  extracted by the same model.
- Memory off, custom instructions off, web search off, every connector off.
- Note the model and the effort as the window shows them, and the time you
  start.

## Steps

1. Open the chat and attach the two files.
2. Paste the opening message below, as it is.
3. After each pass, type `continue` and nothing else.
   - If it asks what something is for, or which reading you prefer, reply:
     "Please follow the brief: make your best judgement and record it as a
     close call in the log."
   - Do not correct its content. If a format problem stops it, ask it to fix
     the format only.
4. **After every pass, download the three files,** and copy that pass's message,
   with the time, into one text file. A Temporary Chat cannot be reopened. The
   files are `bechky_2020_corpus.json`, `bechky_2020_voice_gloss.json` and
   `bechky_2020_extraction_log.md`. Keep each pass's downloads apart, with their
   names as downloaded.
5. If you must stop, stop between passes.
6. When the last pass is done and the three final files are downloaded, the
   chat's work is over. Put nothing else into it. If you want to ask it about
   the brief, as after Pratt's run, ask only after the final downloads, and
   say so when you bring the files.
7. Open no other chat on Bechky for now: no review chat, and no second run.

## The opening message

Paste it as it is:

```
I've attached two files: EXTRACTION_BRIEF.md and a PDF of a published research paper.

Read the brief in full first, then the whole paper, then carry out Pass 0 as the brief describes. After each pass, stop and wait for me to type "continue".

Work only from the attached PDF. Do not search the web, and set aside anything you know about this paper or its author from elsewhere.

paper_id: bechky_2020
citation: Bechky, B. A. 2020. Evaluative spillovers from technological change: The effects of "DNA envy" on occupational practices in forensic science. Administrative Science Quarterly, 65(3): 606–643.
```

## What to bring back

- the three final files, and the files of every pass;
- the text file with each pass's message and its time;
- the model and the effort as the window showed them, and the start and end
  times;
- the Bechky PDF, to check page references and wording. It stays out of the
  folder.

## What happens next

1. The files are validated and the audit is run. Nothing from this goes back
   into any chat.
2. Your coverage check: two hours, section by section. You list the passages
   that produced no fragment or no claim, by page and paragraph only, with no
   reasons.
3. The coverage session: a fresh ChatGPT 5.6 Temporary Chat, with the coverage
   message and five files. Its steps come when it is due.
4. The wording check, and the freeze as `bechky_2020_v1.json`.
5. The second coding, by Opus 5.5 in four chats (A, A2, V, B), once the corpus
   is frozen.
