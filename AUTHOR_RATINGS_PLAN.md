# The author's own worth for the wager readings of Bechky (2020): T34

Fixed on 14 Oct 2026, before anything went to the author. Its text was not
changed after; the changes made since are set out at its foot, dated, with their
reasons.

## What it asks

How the paper's author rates the worth of the 14 Bechky readings of the wager
test (T29, 11 Oct), not knowing which set-up wrote which; how her worth sits
beside the PI's of 11 Oct for the same readings; and which of the readings'
points are new to her. It is described, not tested.

## What she gets

- A short email from the PI.
- `readings_of_bechky_2020.docx`, written by `build_author_readings.py` with
  the seed 20261016: a short note on what the readings are and what is asked,
  the wager test's scale word for word, the 14 readings numbered 1 to 14 in an
  order shuffled by the seed, each exactly as on the PI's sheet of 11 Oct
  (stage2/worth/worth_sheet.xlsx), and a table she may fill in instead of
  writing. Nothing in it shows which chat or set-up wrote a reading.
- Nothing else: not the key, not the PI's ratings, not the markers' marks, no
  other file of the project.

## The two questions

For as many readings as she has time for, in the order given:

1. Worth, 0 to 3, on the wager test's scale.
2. One of "new to me", "I had considered it" or "it is in the paper".

A word of comment where she likes. The note says that all fourteen take about
20 minutes, and that any number is welcome.

## What will be reported, and nothing else

For the readings she rates:

1. Her worth by arm: system (the generation chat, chat 23: 4 readings),
   middle (the reading chat, chat 26: 5) and plain (the plain chat, chat 27:
   5), as means and as counts of 0, 1, 2 and 3, beside the PI's worth for the
   same readings (stage2/worth/worth_sheet_filled_2.xlsx; B06 there was rated
   late, after the PI had been told its arm).
2. Reading by reading: on how many she and the PI give the same worth, and on
   how many they are within one point.
3. How many she rates 2 or more, and how many of those she marks "new to me".
4. Her second answers, counted by arm.

No test of significance: one rater, and 4, 5 and 5 readings. No other cut, and
no choice among her answers. If she rates only some, the figures are for
those, and say how many; in the shuffled order a first part is a fair sample,
though a thin one.

## How her answers are used

- Her reply is filed as she sent it, and goes to nobody. The key,
  `author_ratings_key.json`, is used to score it and goes to nobody.
- Her words are read only to understand her ratings. Nothing she writes is
  quoted, and she is not named, without her leave; without it, the proposal
  gives counts only.
- If her reply comes by Wed 21 Oct, the figures join the freeze of 22 Oct. If
  it comes later, they go in as a note; if none comes, the proposal goes
  without them. Nothing waits on it.

## Limits, known now

- One author, one paper and 14 readings: an illustration, not a test.
- An author has a stake in her own account: she may rate readings that
  contest it lower, or be generous to them.
- The blinding to arm is partial: the set-ups write in different forms (the
  generation chat's readings, for one, state their wager in so many words), as
  they did when the PI rated them.
- She knows the field beyond the paper, so her worth may take in what her own
  data show; the second question and her words keep some of that apart.
- The PI's worth of 11 Oct was declared not blind, so the comparison is
  between an outside reader and the PI, not between two blind readers.
- She is not among the special issue's eight editors (its call for proposals).

## Changes after the plan was fixed

**14 Oct, before the file was sent.** The PI's email to the author adds that her
answers will be used only to assess these readings for the proposal, and not to
train any AI model. So that this holds: her reply stays with the PI, who passes
on only her numbers and her second answers, reading by reading, and these are
what is filed and scored, in place of her reply as she sent it; her words are
read by the PI alone, to understand her ratings. Nothing of hers goes into an AI
service that may train on it, and the project trains no model. Nothing else in
the plan changes.

**15 Oct, before Pratt's file was sent.** The plan is extended to Pratt (2000):
his 15 readings of the wager test, by the same design, questions, scale and
promises (the change of 14 Oct included, for him as for her), in
`readings_of_pratt_2000.docx`, written by `build_author_readings.py
--paper=pratt` with the seed 20261017; its key is
`author_ratings_key_pratt.json`. What will be reported is as above, for each
paper on its own, the two set side by side and never pooled: on Pratt the arms
are system (the generation chat, chat 22: 5 readings), middle (the reading chat,
chat 24: 5) and plain (the plain chat, chat 25: 5), beside the PI's worth of 11
Oct, where P12 and P14 were rated late, after the PI had been told their arm.
The limits above hold for him as for her; and on Pratt the markers' agreement
fell below the bar (kappa 0.593), so no check of the readings' facts as strong
as Bechky's stands beside his worth. Nothing else in the plan changes.
